Learn the market without risking a cent

F.O.M.O. Shield is a stock market simulator. You research real companies, place simulated orders with virtual money, and watch what your own decisions would have done — before they can cost you anything.

No real money. No brokerage account. No advice.

Be clear about what this is

Simulated money only

Every balance, order and profit inside the app is virtual. Nothing you do here reaches a real market, and no result is a prediction of a real one.

Not a broker

We hold no funds, execute no trades and connect to no brokerage. There is nothing to deposit and nothing to withdraw.

Not investment advice

Scores, verdicts and articles are educational material. They are not recommendations to buy or sell anything, and we are not registered investment advisors.

What you actually do in the app

Real market data from real companies, and a safe place to be wrong in.

Research a company before you touch it

Open any listed company and get the picture in one screen — current price, the move today, and a price chart from one day out to its whole history.

  • A financial strength score, with the reasoning behind it spelled out
  • Every metric explained in plain language, not textbook jargon
  • Company histories written from public disclosures, in the Company Encyclopedia

Place an order and live with the consequences

Market and limit orders, position sizing as a share of the cash you have, and a 0.5% brokerage commission modelled the way a real one would bite.

  • Limit orders sit and wait, then notify you when they fill
  • Every order is confirmed on a summary sheet before it goes through
  • Your holdings, average cost and unrealised P&L are tracked for you

Find out what your portfolio is really doing

Portfolio Health scores the shape of your holdings — diversification, concentration, sector balance and stability — and then explains each number instead of just showing it.

  • See how much of your portfolio rests on a single company
  • See which sectors you have quietly doubled up on
  • Asset allocation broken down holding by holding

Catch your own habits, not just your results

Market Simulation runs a portfolio through a compressed market period with weekly funding, dividends and commissions. The Psychology Meter then scores the strategy behind your trades rather than the luck in them.

  • Concentration, cash buffer and ETF exposure, scored as behaviour
  • A full trade breakdown: every fee, dividend and top-up accounted for
  • Sessions keep running while you are away, and survive a restart
