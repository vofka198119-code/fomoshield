Delete Your Account — F.O.M.O. Shield

Last updated: September 18, 2026

F.O.M.O. Shield (developer: Volodymyr Oleksiienko) lets you request account deletion directly inside the app — no separate request needs to be sent to us.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the F.O.M.O. Shield app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt titled "Delete Account?".

What happens next

Your account is scheduled for deletion, not erased instantly. You have 14 days to change your mind — simply sign back in during that window and the app will offer to restore your account exactly as it was.

If you don't restore it within 14 days, your account and all associated data are permanently and irreversibly erased.
