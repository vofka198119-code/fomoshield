6. Stress Test Simulations

The Stress Test feature runs an accelerated simulation of market conditions, built from historical market data and behavioral patterns and compressed into a much shorter timeframe than real markets would take. Its purpose is educational: to help you notice your own decision-making patterns and mistakes (e.g. panic-selling, chasing hype, poor diversification) in a controlled, low-stakes setting.

Stress Test results, scenarios, and any feedback derived from them (including FS Scores generated during a session) are not instructions or signals for real-world investment decisions. They reflect how a simulated market, built from historical patterns, behaved during that session — not a prediction of how any real market will behave in the future. Real markets can, and often do, behave differently from any simulation. Nothing in the Stress Test feature should be treated as a recommendation to take any specific action with real money.
